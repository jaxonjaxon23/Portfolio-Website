# Jaxon Stickler — CV

Designer/Artist · b. 1998, Australia

Designer and artist working across objects, bodies, and sites — taking projects from concept through to finished artefact. Combines advanced digital tools (Rhino, Grasshopper, Gravity Sketch, 3D printing) with hands-on fabrication across patternmaking and material experimentation, producing sculptural objects, installations, wearables, and speculative artefacts for exhibitions, studios, and independent commissions. Available for design, fabrication, and collaborative projects.
